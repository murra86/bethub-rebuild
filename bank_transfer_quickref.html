Money moves — quick reference

Golden rule: the Bankroll row in the tool = the bankroll bank app, every day, to the cent.

One hop to your books. Two hops via the helpers — so two taps, until the transfer door lands.

Real-world moveTaps in the tool
Personal money in (e.g. the $3,000) Bankroll → Top-up float1 tap
Take profit home Bankroll → Return float1 tap
Bankroll → your book Book → Deposit, “Tim’s float”1 tap
Your book → bankroll (always withdraw to the bankroll account) Book → Withdraw1 tap
Bankroll → Sarie → her book (her float empty) Bankroll → Return float then Her book → Deposit, “fresh bank money”2 taps
Her held cash → home to bankroll Her float → Return float then Bankroll → Top-up float2 taps
Never: a bare one-tap deposit into a helper’s book when their float is empty — it hides the money leaving your bankroll.  Mis-booked? Money movements ledger → Reverse → redo.
