The idea: one small list of the promos you're holding today, each tagged either for a specific race or floating (any race, limited uses). This list is what the watcher reads to grade the whole card hands-free.

The choice

RECOMMENDED

A "Today's promos" list

One place you keep the promos you hold — each "for race X" or "floating, N uses". The watcher reads the whole list, so it can grade every race and find the best home for a floating promo. Needed for the hands-free vision.

Pin on each race screen

You arm a promo on a race and it sticks to that race — like today's top bar, but persistent. Simpler, but the tool never has a full picture of what you hold, so a floating promo can't be "spent where it's best" — you'd hunt for it yourself.

Design intent:

1. The list is the source of truth the watcher reads. Race-pinned promos grade their race automatically; floating promos get a "best spot" suggestion across the card. That "best spot" only works because the tool holds the promo independently of any race you're looking at.

2. Adding a promo reuses your catalogue — you pick a saved promo type, then just say "for this race" or "floating, how many uses, expires when". Nothing new to define each time.

3. The top bar still works as today for the race you're actively on — this list sits alongside it, it doesn't replace your normal arming.

4. Tracking a floating promo's "uses left / expires" is the one genuinely new piece of bookkeeping — that's the next decision after this one.
