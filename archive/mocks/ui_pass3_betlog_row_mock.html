One choice for you — where the P&L column sits

RECOMMENDED

Far right, after State

"Won → +$120". Reads left-to-right as outcome-then-money, and it's a clean add — your locked 9-column order doesn't move at all, P&L just tacks on the end.

Right after Stake

Groups the money together (stake next to result). But it pushes Promo/State right and technically reorders the columns you locked — more disruptive to the layout you're used to.

Design intent:

1. Nothing existing moves (recommended option) — the 9 columns you locked stay put; P&L is a 10th on the end and the 🎁 lives inside the Promo column that's already there.

2. Both are display-only — the P&L and the insurance flag are already on every bet; nothing new gets calculated or stored.

3. The row gets a little wider with the 10th column. On the desktop tool that's fine; on a narrow window it scrolls sideways rather than squashing (as the board already does).

4. Icon is a placeholder — 🎁 gift, or a 🛡 shield if you'd rather it read as "protected". Your pick.
