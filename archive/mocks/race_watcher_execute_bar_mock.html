Where the execute bar (Q1) comes in

The bar is the +5% line that decides "worth it" — it's what turns Rapid Vision's projected EV into a STRONG rather than a shrug. It never shows on the screen; it just sets where the Calls flip. One global line = one hidden dial behind every Call, every promo. That's the choice: one dial, or a separate dial per promo type. On this row-level view you'd never see the difference — it's purely how the Calls get computed.

Design intent:

1. One new column ("Call"), nothing else moves. Every existing column stays exactly where it is and where you locked it. The watcher rides on data already on the row.

2. The reasons live on hover/tap, not on the row — tapping a Call shows +2.3% now → +8.5% @ 2.70 · pool coherent · trend agrees · 8m. The row stays clean; the detail's one tap away.

3. Quiet by default. Runners with nothing to say get a faint dot, not a pill — the eye only catches the STRONG/MOD/LEAVE that matter.

4. If even one column is too much, the fallback is no column at all — a coloured dot on the # cell. But a labelled Call reads faster than a dot you have to decode. My lean is the column.
