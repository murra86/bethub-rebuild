What changed since v1

1 · Denser where it counts. Runner rows tightened so ten runners plus the board fit on a laptop screen; small fields simply take less height (the table only ever grows with the field). The race list now shows twelve races at once, same trick — thinner rows, nothing smaller to read.

2 · Race-type filters, thoroughbred by default. The three toggles at the top of the race list switch codes on and off; the app opens with only Thoro on. Each race carries its colour-coded letter — T, H, G — so with two codes on you can tell them apart at a glance (the letter does the work; colour is the accelerator). The race header repeats the tag so you always know what you're looking at.

3 · Top bar on a grid. The three rows now hang off one label column — Person / Books / Promo — with a single spacing unit everywhere, so chips line up instead of drifting. Stake and min-EV dials moved up beside the person row, which saves a whole row of height. Balances stay on the book chips.
